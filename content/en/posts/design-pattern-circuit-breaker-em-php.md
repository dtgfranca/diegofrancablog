The Circuit Breaker is a design pattern used in software development. It is used to detect failures and encapsulate the logic to prevent cascading failures. When this pattern is applied, our system becomes fault-tolerant and resilient — that is, when a service our application communicates with becomes unavailable, our system can deal with it.
